# Methodology and Evidence Classes

ALLAMA ABSOLUTE separates four kinds of evidence so that a reproducible calculation, a traditional interpretation, an AI summary and an observed outcome are never presented as the same thing.

## Deterministic computation

Figure parity, Daughter transposition, Arabic normalization, Abjad arithmetic, astronomical coordinates, cryptographic hashes and statistical summaries are functions of recorded inputs. The same inputs and methodology version should reproduce the same output. Record timestamps, time zones, locations, conventions and source versions where they affect a result. A range check is weaker than a known-answer check against an independent authority; [astronomy validation](ASTRONOMY_VALIDATION.md) states the present fixture coverage and its JPL data gap.

## Traditional interpretation

Figure qualities, house meanings, planetary correspondences and school rules are attributed claims. Record the school, rule version and provenance when available. The weighted testimony score and contradiction penalties are internal interpretive indices, **not** calibrated probabilities of a future event. An opposing rule remains visible as a contradiction rather than being silently averaged away. A source entry or document annotation is traceability, not proof that a traditional claim is scientifically valid.

## AI synthesis

AI is optional and operates after deterministic analysis. It may explain, compare and summarize evidence already in the case; it must not invent figures, arithmetic, source citations, astronomical values, outcomes or observations. An AI answer is prose from the configured provider, not an independent measurement or empirical validation. In a blind session, the sealed analysis is generated without the hidden question, and the reveal is a separate step.

## Empirical evidence

The empirical layer begins only with a prediction fixed **before** the outcome and a subsequently recorded, independently checkable result. Trial locks can verify that a saved prediction payload was not changed after creation. Record the outcome, timing, scoring rubric and missing data. Calibration, accuracy, rule-survival and ablation summaries require a meaningful sample and should expose their denominator; a small or selected set of cases does not establish general predictive accuracy. Human-versus-system comparisons need the same blinded questions, outcome definitions and scoring for both sides.

## Research workflow

1. Record the question, cast method, timestamp, location and methodology version.
2. Compute the figures and other deterministic values, retaining raw inputs and derivation.
3. Apply attributed rules and show both supporting and contradicting testimony.
4. Save a specific, falsifiable prediction and its lock before learning the outcome.
5. After the outcome, score against the predeclared criterion and run error analysis and calibration without changing the historical prediction.

The 777-universe route and Deep Search organize retrieval and symbolic evidence; multiple agreeing views derived from the same input are correlated, not independent confirmations. A council lens is a modeled interpretive perspective, not a claim that a historical person examined this case. If a reference dataset is absent, mark the result unverified or data-blocked rather than filling the gap with a generated value.

## Boundaries

Remote-state output is ranked symbolic hypothesis, never direct perception, GPS measurement, mind reading or proof about a hidden person or place. Health, legal, financial and safety-critical questions can be categorized for research, but symbolic output cannot replace professional diagnosis, legal adjudication, financial advice, emergency services or direct evidence. Human review and real-world verification take priority.

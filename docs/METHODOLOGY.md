# Methodology and Evidence Classes

ALLAMA ABSOLUTE uses four separate evidence classes.

## Deterministic computation

Examples include parity addition, Daughter transposition, Arabic normalization, Abjad sums, cryptographic hashes, astronomical coordinates and statistical summaries. These results must be reproducible from their inputs.

## Traditional interpretation

Figure quality, house meaning, planetary correspondence and school rules belong here. They may vary by tradition. Conflicts must remain visible instead of being silently merged.

## AI synthesis

AI is optional. It may summarize and compare evidence already present in a case, but it is not permitted to invent figures, arithmetic, sources, astronomical values, outcomes, or observations.

## Empirical evidence

This layer begins only after predictions and real outcomes are recorded. It includes accuracy, calibration, rule survival, ablation tests and human-versus-system comparisons.

## Confidence

The current traditional confidence score is an internal weighted-testimony index with contradiction penalties. It is not a calibrated probability of future events. Empirical calibration is stored and reported separately.

## Remote-state discipline

Remote-state output is represented as ranked symbolic hypotheses. It is never labeled direct perception, GPS measurement, verified observation, mind reading, or proof about a hidden person or place.

## High-stakes discipline

Health, legal, financial and safety-critical questions can be categorized for research, but symbolic outputs cannot replace professional diagnosis, legal adjudication, financial guarantees, emergency services, or direct evidence.
